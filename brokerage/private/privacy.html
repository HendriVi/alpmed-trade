Privacy and enquiries

Private setup — public enquiries are not open. Use synthetic business details while testing. No patient or health information.

What this service records

The forms record business contact details, organisation, equipment requirements or offers, country, budget or asking price and the details you choose to provide. Supplier submissions are private by default. Contact details are never included in the public catalogue.

Purpose and access

Enquiries are stored to assess potential equipment matches and respond to the person requesting contact. The owner dashboard requires the authorised owner’s ChatGPT sign-in. This site uses no advertising analytics or marketing subscriptions.

Hosting and retention

The application uses ChatGPT Sites with Cloudflare Workers and D1 storage. Hosting location, international transfer safeguards, retention periods and the responsible legal controller must be confirmed in the final notice before public collection begins. Rate-limit identifiers are derived from a daily salted hash of the connection IP and are not stored as raw IP addresses in the application database. Hosting providers may separately process security logs.

Before public launch

The operator’s legal name, postal address, contact email, applicable legal basis, rights procedure and approved retention policy are still required. No demonstration stock is offered for sale. Submitting an enquiry does not create a purchase, brokerage contract or payment obligation.
