# Illustrative transaction — NOT an actual lead or confirmed stock

Romanian clinic requests an ultrasound system with budget EUR 20,000. German refurbisher supplies eligible and technically documented unit at EUR 18,000. Parties agree supplier-paid **10% brokerage fee**, or EUR 1,800. Broker estimates EUR 200 administrative/partner expenses plus EUR 300 risk reserve, leaving **EUR 1,300 expected deal contribution** (not final net profit, taxes excluded). Independent escrow holds buyer purchase funds; supplier ships, installs through authorized local partner; post-acceptance escrow releases funds and fee according to the agreed terms. No broker-owned stock or broker-held escrow. This is a numerical illustration; the actual commission, payment structure and legal roles require agreement and verification.
