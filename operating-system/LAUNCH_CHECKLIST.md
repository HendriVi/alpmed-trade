# Launch checklist — actual minimum necessary

**Do not create an extra spreadsheet, business plan or app. They already exist.**

- [ ] Choose actual operating entity and business email; fill privacy and site legal details.
- [ ] Confirm Swiss/EU brokerage role and Romanian ANMDMR second-hand device requirements with qualified adviser / authorized Romanian partner.
- [ ] Confirm seller of record, installation/service partner, customs/VAT, insurance, warranties and fee payer.
- [ ] Confirm a regulated independent escrow provider will accept the planned transaction and broker disbursements.
- [ ] Give a file-aware development agent this ZIP and `RUN_WITH_AI.md`; let it install, bind Cloudflare D1 and deploy **after explicit permission**.
- [ ] Set owner password as secret. Complete anti-bot/security review. Test website forms and private records. Publish contact, privacy and legal information.
- [ ] Run buyer and supplier research stages on official sites; store *unverified* public research without sending bulk outreach.
- [ ] Obtain first opted-in buyer RFQ and first supplier-confirmed eligible offer.
- [ ] Create one positive-contribution deal. Document all stages; no payment handling by broker.
- [ ] Once first deal closes, review founder hours and automate the single largest repeated friction.

**Acceptance result:** public inquiry → database record → private owner qualification → reviewed offer → candidate match → positive-profit deal → evidence-led progress → recorded fee receipt. It is **not** a success if any of these are mocked in production.
