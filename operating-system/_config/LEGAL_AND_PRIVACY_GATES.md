# Canonical legal and privacy gates — READ BEFORE ALL TRANSACTIONS

**Not legal advice. Verify current rules with qualified experts before live use.**

1. **Romania second-hand use approval:** Order 2219/2022 Article 8 provides for ANMDMR performance evaluation and an `aviz de utilizare` for relevant second-hand devices. It also establishes a requirement for at least three years of remaining normal service life at first introduction to the Romanian market, with equipment age documents. ANMDMR's published clarifications explain cases in which a previously issued approval need not be repeated. Check applicability per exact device and history. Source: https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/FormaPrintabila/00000G0P8U1UI6JTWIL04DS0QZ4DV5B5 ; https://www.anm.ro/dispozitive-medicale/anunturi-importante-dispozitive-medicale/
2. **Who is the legally authorized seller/distributor/importer?** ANMDMR identifies import, distribution, installation and maintenance as controlled activities requiring relevant approvals in Romania. EU movement does not automatically waive authorization. Determine legal role of each party, location of the broker, invoicing and VAT treatment. Source: https://www.anm.ro/dispozitive-medicale/avize-de-functionare/
3. **Classification, conformity and technical record:** CE / EU MDR 2017/745, serial number, UDI where applicable, service history, safety/performance inspection, software licences, recalls, OEM support, accessories, manufacturer service-life limit and warranties. 'Refurbishment' may affect regulatory classification/responsibility. Obtain expert confirmation. No seller claims based on website text alone.
4. **Escrow:** Funds held by an independent eligible payment provider, not by the broker. Confirm the particular medical-device category, buyer/seller countries, broker commission, payment rails, dispute procedure, release criteria and charge allocation in writing. Do not assume all markets are supported.
5. **Shipping:** The seller/qualified partner coordinates de-installation, decontamination, packaging, freight insurance, Incoterms, commissioning, required certificates and after-sales support. Buyer/seller contract must assign responsibilities clearly.
6. **Privacy:** Do not collect patient health information. Complete `app/public/privacy.html` using the REAL legal company name, contact, controller details, lawful basis, retention, hosting/transfers, rights and service-provider terms before publishing. HTTPS, access control, secure password, backups, anti-bot controls and data retention plan required.
7. **Marketing:** Confirm applicable Romanian Law 506/2004 electronic marketing restrictions and GDPR before outreach. Treat scraped emails and bulk cold emails as unsafe defaults. Prioritize opt-in RFQs, existing contacts, partner introductions and explicit permission. No mass automated sending.
8. **Geography:** Lebanon, Turkey and Middle Eastern markets are disabled until their import permissions, used medical device restrictions, payment availability and sanctions/customs checks are completed.
