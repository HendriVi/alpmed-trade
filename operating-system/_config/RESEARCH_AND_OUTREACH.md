# Research discipline / AI lead generation

**Prospect ≠ buyer.** A clinic offering ultrasound examinations does not prove purchase intent. Store evidence type, dated URL and verification timestamp. Prefer official clinic webpages, expansion press releases, procurement notices and partner referrals. A directly confirmed RFQ is the only verified buying intent. No fabricated emails, names, sourcing, pricing or purchasing signals.

**Catalog listing ≠ verified stock.** Confirm quote expiration, serial, year, condition, servicing history, spare parts, warranty and availability with supplier. A 'possible match' does not create an order.

**Conversion channels in priority order:** (1) referrals from Romanian service/install partners with buyer permission; (2) buyer opt-in via website RFQ; (3) approved individual outreach where law permits. Do not auto-send unsolicited marketing emails.

**Agent outputs:** source link, date, country, organization, relevance, demand/supply evidence, contact page, confidence, information gaps and 'not contacted / contacted with consent / replied'. Never write a person's private phone or email from an unauthorized source. Write research output to stage folder, update the app upon verification.

**Automation status:** Public form capture and candidate matching are coded. General internet AI research is NOT yet connected: it requires a web-enabled execution service and scheduling/permissions. Use `RUN_WITH_AI.md` in a file-aware Work/coding agent for an on-demand research pass; connect scheduled research only after verifying costs, legal basis and quality.
