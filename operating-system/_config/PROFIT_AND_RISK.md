# Canonical economics and risk rules

Brokerage revenue = device transaction value × **disclosed contractually agreed fee rate**. Your net contribution = brokerage revenue − brokerage-specific costs − risk reserve. Compute conservatively and in EUR, excluding taxes and VAT accounting. Example: EUR 20,000 equipment, 10% fee, EUR 200 operational costs, EUR 300 reserve => EUR 1,500 expected contribution. No predetermined required fee percentage: test 5%, 10%, 20% or negotiated fixed fees. The app REJECTS non-positive expected contribution.

Seller and buyer must be clear who pays the brokerage fee and how escrow distributes it. The website must not suggest the broker owns or warrants equipment where it does not. Do not hide commissions or invent markup by holding client money.

As the first deals develop, record founder minutes per deal and customer acquisition expenses; no revenue is 'profit' until costs, disputes, taxes and founder time are considered. No inventory, no self-funded deposits or freight payments. An escrow provider's funds are never your working capital. Skip deals with uncertain compliance, technical condition, service support or negative expected contribution.
